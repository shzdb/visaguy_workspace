GROUND FACTS (verified by the orchestrator against the live bench — do NOT re-derive, do NOT
argue, do NOT "improve" on these):

- Your backoff used `time.sleep()` inside the worker. This is REJECTED. A sleeping RQ worker
  is a blocked worker: a handful of rate-limited messages starve the `short` queue for
  everything else on the bench.
- `frappe.enqueue(..., timeout=N)` does NOT delay a job. Verified at
  `frappe/utils/background_jobs.py:160` — `timeout` is passed to RQ as `job_timeout`, the
  maximum EXECUTION duration before the worker kills the job as failed (default 300s from
  `get_queues_timeout()`). It is a kill-switch, not a schedule. Do not use it for backoff.
  It also means your sleep >300s would be killed mid-sleep.
- RQ 1.15.1's `enqueue_in` is NOT usable here: `bench worker` on this bench runs WITHOUT
  `--with-scheduler` (verified in the bench Procfile), so scheduled jobs would never fire.
- Therefore: there is no delayed-enqueue primitive available. Do not invent one.

THE CORRECT MECHANISM ALREADY EXISTS IN THIS APP. Use it.

`waflo` already ships a retry pipeline that is in production:

1. `WhatsApp Message` has custom fields `custom_should_retry` and `custom_retried_message`.
2. `wf_settings.py` already flags messages for retry with:
   `frappe.db.set_value("WhatsApp Message", {"message_id": message_id}, "custom_should_retry", 1)`
3. `scheduler_events` in `waflo/hooks.py` runs `schedule_retry_message` hourly (`0 * * * *`).
4. `schedule_retry_message` selects
   `frappe.get_all("WhatsApp Message", filters={"custom_should_retry": 1, "custom_retried_message": None})`
   and calls `retry_message(doc.as_dict(), queued=False)` for each.
5. `retry_message` in `waflo/waflo/messaging/retry_message.py` reconstructs the send from the
   WhatsApp Message record's fields: `to`, `template`, `body_param`,
   `template_header_parameters`, `buttons`, `reference_doctype`, `reference_name`,
   `custom_is_flow`.
6. The `custom_retried_message: None` filter means each message is retried at most once —
   that is your natural retry bound. Do not add a competing counter.
